1. Introduction

Welcome to Sanjivani Farm & Resort! When you visit our website, book a stay, or spend time with us at the resort, you trust us with your personal information. We truly value that trust. This page is here to explain, in simple terms, what information we collect, why we need it, and how we keep it safe.

2. Data Controller

Sanjivani Farm & Resort is responsible for the information you share with us. If you ever have a question about your data or want to talk to us about your privacy, you can always reach out to our team using the contact details at the bottom of this page.

3. The Data We Collect

To make your booking smooth and your stay comfortable, we collect a few necessary details. When you reach out to us or make a reservation, we ask for basic information like your name, phone number, and email address.

When you arrive at the resort, we are required by local hospitality laws to ask for a valid government ID (like your Aadhar card or Driving License). If you're making payments online, your payment details are securely processed by our trusted payment partners.

We also collect some basic technical information when you browse our website, such as what type of device or browser you're using. This simply helps us make sure the website works well and looks good for everyone.

4. How We Use Your Data

We only use your information when it helps us provide you with a great experience. Mainly, we use it to:

  • Confirm your bookings and send you important updates about your stay.
  • Help you out if you contact us with questions or special requests.
  • Understand how people use our website so we can keep improving it.
  • Comply with local regulations, like keeping a guest register for the Palghar authorities.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy, which means we never sell your personal information. We only share it when absolutely necessary to run the resort properly.

For example, we securely share data with the services that process our payments and the companies that host our website. We also share guest details with local law enforcement if it is required by law. Whenever we do share information with our service providers, we make sure they treat it with the same respect and security that we do.

6. Data Security

Keeping your information safe is a big priority for us. We use secure systems to prevent your details from being lost, accessed by the wrong people, or altered. Only the staff members who actually need to see your information (like our front desk team confirming your booking) have access to it, and they keep it strictly confidential.

7. Data Retention

We only keep your information for as long as we genuinely need it. After your stay is over, we hold onto basic booking and billing details for a certain period because tax and hospitality laws require us to do so. Once that time has passed, we safely delete your records.

8. Your Legal Rights

It's your data, and you have rights over it. You are always welcome to ask us what information we have about you. If anything is incorrect, just let us know and we'll fix it. You can also ask us to delete your details if we no longer have a legal reason to keep them.
