1. General Timings

We want you to have a relaxed and comfortable time at the farm. To help us prepare for your visit and ensure a smooth experience for all guests, please keep these timings in mind:

  • Check-in time: 12:00 Noon
  • Check-out time: 10:00 AM

If you need a little extra time before heading home, late check-out is available at an additional charge (₹500 to ₹1000 extra per hour, depending on availability). Please check with the reception during your stay.

2. Meal Schedule

Our kitchen prepares fresh, delicious meals for you. Please join us at the dining area during these hours:

  • Lunch (1:00 PM to 2:00 PM): Includes Dal Tadka, Steam Rice, Chapati or Bhakri, Chicken Curry, Salad, Papad, Pickle, and a Sweet. For vegetarians, we serve one type of vegetable dish along with the sweet.
  • Evening Snacks (5:00 PM to 6:00 PM): Enjoy evening snacks paired with hot tea.
  • Dinner (8:45 PM to 10:00 PM): Includes Dal Fry, Steam Rice, Chapati or Bhakri, Chicken Masala, Chicken Dry, Salad, Papad, Pickle, and a Sweet. For vegetarians, we offer two types of vegetable dishes along with the sweet.
  • Breakfast (9:00 AM to 10:30 AM): Start your day with Egg Burji Pav, Poha, Bread Butter Jam, Misal Pav, and Tea or Coffee.

Please note that room service is not available, so we invite you to enjoy your meals in our designated dining areas.

3. Important Notes

To maintain a safe and pleasant environment for everyone, we kindly request your cooperation with the following policies:

  • Alcohol strictly prohibited 🚫
  • Hookah strictly prohibited 🚫 🚭
  • Please take care of your personal belongings. The management cannot be held responsible for misplaced items.
  • We advise against wearing expensive clothes or jewelry in the swimming pool, as the chlorine water may cause damage. The management is not responsible for such damages.
  • Any damage done to the resort property will incur applicable charges.
  • Please remember to deposit your room keys at the reception at the time of check-out. A fine of ₹500 will be applied for lost or unreturned keys.
  • We love our gardens, and we ask that you do not pluck fruits or flowers from the plants ❌

4. Resort Rules

We want everyone to have a joyful and peaceful stay. Here are a few simple rules to keep the farm enjoyable for all guests:

  • Swimming Pool: Open from 9:30 AM to 7:30 PM.
  • Boating & Kayaking: Available from 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM.
  • Drinking and food are not allowed in the rooms or the swimming pool area, helping us keep these spaces clean for everyone.
  • Please be mindful and respectful of other guests so everyone can enjoy their time here. Misbehaving or disturbing others will not be tolerated.

5. Cancellation Policy

We understand that plans can change. Here is how our cancellation process works:

  • You will receive a 100% refund of the stay amount if your booking is cancelled up to 7 days before your check-in date.
  • There is a 0% refund if the booking is cancelled within 7 days of your check-in date.
